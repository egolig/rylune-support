# Rylune support

Rylune is a personal period and symptom journal for iPhone. The app is currently in development.

**Developer:** Ege Yergun  
**Support and privacy contact:** [egeyergunn@gmail.com](mailto:egeyergunn@gmail.com)

For help, include the app version, iOS version and a description of the issue. Please do not send your health journal, medical records, passwords or Apple verification codes. Support cannot provide medical advice.

- [Privacy policy](PRIVACY.md)
- [Apple Standard End User License Agreement](https://www.apple.com/legal/internet-services/itunes/dev/stdeula/)

Rylune records your observations and offers estimates for personal organization. It does not diagnose, treat, confirm ovulation or provide contraception. Seek qualified healthcare advice for medical decisions.
